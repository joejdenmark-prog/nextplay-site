How it works 1 1 Send us a clip Gameplay, a devlog, a trailer cut. Anything up to two minutes that shows what your game is like. 2 2 We check in with you We look at the clip, confirm you're happy for us to post it, and agree how you want to be credited. 3 3 You're on the app at launch Your clip is live when NextPlay opens, with your name and a link to your game, so players can find you. Your game, your terms What you get, and what stays yours • Credit and a link. Your name, your game and a link to your page or store go with every clip. • It stays yours. You keep all rights to your clip and your game. We only post it with your say-so. • Take it down any time. Ask and we'll remove it, no questions. • A founding developer spot. Early access, a founding badge, and a direct line to the two people building NextPlay. Submit your clip Send us one clip of your game. It takes about two minutes.
